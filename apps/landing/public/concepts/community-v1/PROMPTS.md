# Community Gallery Prompts

## Shared project-render direction

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: landscape community-card render. Create one physically plausible, low-power ESP32 maker product in a warm lived-in home or desk environment. Use the same Makeable industrial language across the set: warm off-white 3D-printed enclosure, soft graphite display inset, small practical vents, visible but tidy USB-C power, rounded manufacturable edges, honest seams, and restrained cyan/cobalt/coral UI accents. Frame the product in a three-quarter front view from slightly above with a natural 50 mm product-photography look, realistic window or desk lighting, and enough environmental context to explain its purpose. The housing must visibly accommodate the described sensor or control; do not expose raw boards. No brand marks, readable prose, fake certifications, batteries, mains wiring, motors, pumps, heaters, relays, high-current loads, level shifters, loose wires, floating components, exploded diagrams, multiple versions, white-background ecommerce styling, or neon sci-fi treatment.

## Window Air Monitor

Create a compact tabletop air and comfort monitor beside a bright apartment window and a healthy houseplant. Give it a wide dark inset display with simple generic cloud, thermometer, humidity-drop, and leaf status symbols; a small lower vent for the environmental sensor; side ventilation; and a tidy USB-C cable. The screen may contain icons and indicator dots only, with no readable words or numeric claims. Use pale morning light, realistic desk wood, gentle greenery, and a warm off-white enclosure with subtle 3D-print texture.

## Pet Water Reminder

Create a small USB-C-powered pet water reminder placed next to a stainless-steel water bowl in a calm home feeding area. Give it a compact upright warm off-white enclosure, a small dark display with one simple cyan water-drop icon, a practical front grille or indicator area, and believable sensor placement without loose wiring. Use warm natural light, quiet wood and neutral materials, and a scale that looks buildable around an ESP32, a verified water-level input, and a simple indicator output.

## Quiet Door Chime

Create a slim, wall-mounted quiet arrival notifier beside a residential wooden door. Give it a warm off-white 3D-printed enclosure with a small dark display showing one simple amber bell icon, a discreet lower status grille, a plausible seam, and no exposed wiring. The concept should read as a magnetic contact sensor plus a gentle visual notification, powered by a safe low-power ESP32 design. Use warm entryway light, realistic plaster and wood texture, and a clean three-quarter view.
