## Full-page UI concept

References, in order: the four selected renders above, then
`/apps/landing/public/makeable-logo-tight.webp`.

> Use case: website design concept. Create a polished tall, browserless desktop
> landing-page mockup for Makeable.build with three decisive sections in this
> exact order. First, a static full-viewport flagship hero using the exact Ember
> image as a full-bleed background, with minimal transparent navigation and
> simple lower-left copy: “Feed Ember Tokens.”, “A desk pet that grows with every
> Claude and Codex token you burn.”, “Pre-order Ember”, and “Explore builds”.
> Keep Ember unobstructed on the right. Second, a midnight-navy “Create your own
> build.” section with the supporting sentence “Tell us what you want to make.
> We’ll turn it into a kit, a guide, and a working build.” and one massive
> warm-white rounded composer reading “Describe what you want to build…” with a
> thin luminous cobalt-to-violet-to-coral edge, soft blue/pink aurora, plus icon,
> and dark circular arrow. Third, a warm-cream “Builds to get you started” section
> with “Pick a project. Make it yours.” and exactly three equal cards using the
> supplied Study Desk Companion, Plant Companion, and Motion Light images in
> that order. Card descriptions are “A little companion built for focused desk
> time.”, “Give your plant a way to tell you what it needs.”, and “A warm light
> that wakes up when you walk by.” End with a minimal dark footer. Use a bold,
> friendly modern grotesk, midnight navy, warm cream, cobalt blue, coral, and
> restrained hot pink only in the composer glow. Preserve the supplied product
> and logo identity. No browser chrome, debug banner, Roblox/Lovable branding,
> scroll animation, 3D exploded view, extra cards, fake metrics, testimonials,
> awards, pricing, newsletters, floating glass panels, or unrelated sections.
