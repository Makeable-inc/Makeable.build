CLAUDE BURNER 1.0.8 — LIVELY THREE-SECOND EMBER LOOPS

1. Flash Claude_Burner_1.0.8_CYD_Firmware.bin to the connected E32R28T/CYD.
   The currently connected board was flashed and verified during this build.
2. Open Claude Burner-1.0.8-arm64.dmg and drag Claude Burner to Applications.
3. This prototype is unsigned. Control-click the app and choose Open. If macOS
   blocks it, use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.

WHAT CHANGED IN 1.0.8
- Every emotion now has a longer, seamless three-second animation.
- Ember breathes, bobs, blinks, sways its flame, and reacts more visibly.
- Clouds, stars, ash, sparks, and foreground plants now move with each mood.
- Desktop previews use 144 frames at 48 FPS.
- ESP32 loops use 72 memory-bounded frames at 24 FPS and remain cached until
  the emotion changes; LIFE-only updates do not replace the GIF.
- The protected LIFE HUD and correctly placed NO CONNECTION banner are kept.
- All ten emotions passed upload, loop, render-budget, and cache-replay checks
  on the physical connected non-PSRAM CYD at 24.10-24.28 FPS.
