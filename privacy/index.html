How we handle personal data

Collect less. Protect what matters.

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Makeable collects, uses, discloses, protects, and retains personal data when you use makeable.build, join a waitlist or pilot, place an Ember preorder, or contact us.

Data we collect

  • Identity and contact data: name, email address, phone number, billing address, and shipping address.
  • Order data: selected product colour, quantity, price, currency, order and checkout identifiers, payment status, delivery details, and the consent choices submitted with the order.
  • Payment data: Stripe collects and processes your payment credentials. Makeable does not receive or store your full card number or card security code. We receive limited transaction information needed to confirm and support the order.
  • Waitlist and sign-in data: Google sign-in supplies your name, email address, email-verification status, and a stable account identifier. Google may supply a profile-image URL. Makeable does not store the stable identifier or profile-image URL in new waitlist records, and never stores your Google credential token.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device details, request timestamps, security logs, and basic interaction data generated when you use the site.
  • Communications: messages, support requests, feedback, and your marketing preference.

Why we use personal data

We use personal data only for reasonable, notified purposes, including to:

  • create, process, deliver, refund, and support orders;
  • verify payments, prevent fraud, and protect the site;
  • manage waitlists, pilot access, accounts, and support requests;
  • send transactional messages about an order or service;
  • send product news and marketing only where you have opted in or where otherwise permitted by law, with an unsubscribe option;
  • debug, maintain, and improve our products and services; and
  • meet legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, and dispute obligations.

Consent and your choices

Where consent is required, we ask for it for a stated purpose. Marketing consent is optional and is not required to buy Ember. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time by using an unsubscribe link or contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully and does not stop service or order messages that are still necessary.

Google signup and confirmation cookie

We use Google sign-in to verify a waitlist email and may keep your name to personalize an invitation. We deduplicate records by normalized email address. After a verified signup, Makeable stores a random, HttpOnly confirmation cookie so the same browser can recognize that you are already on the list. It contains no email address, cannot be read by page JavaScript, and expires after one year. Removing cookies means you may need to confirm with Google again.

Who receives personal data

We disclose only what is reasonably needed to:

  • Stripe, which processes checkout, payment, billing, and related fraud prevention under its own privacy notice;
  • Netlify and other hosting, storage, security, communications, and fulfilment providers that process data for us;
  • professional advisers, insurers, auditors, or transaction counterparties where confidentiality protections apply; and
  • courts, regulators, law enforcement, or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use Google sign-in or order data for third-party targeted advertising.

International transfers

Our providers may process personal data outside Singapore or your country. Where required, we use contractual, organizational, or other legally recognized safeguards designed to provide a standard of protection comparable to applicable data-protection requirements.

Retention

We keep personal data only while it is needed for the purposes described above or for a legal or business requirement. Order and transaction records may be retained for tax, accounting, warranty, fraud-prevention, and dispute periods. Waitlist records are kept while they remain relevant to access or product communications. We delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of data when it is no longer needed.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the data and risk. No transmission or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We assess and notify eligible data breaches as required by applicable law.

Access, correction, deletion, and withdrawal

Depending on applicable law, you may ask to access or correct personal data, request deletion, withdraw consent, or ask how data was used or disclosed. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where legally required. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

Children

The site and store are not directed to children who cannot legally consent to the relevant processing. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child provided personal data without appropriate permission.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy as the product, providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised version and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request fresh consent.
